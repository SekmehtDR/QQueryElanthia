Hi, I'm Fennick!

Halfling, Bard, keeper of an alarmingly heavy satchel

Where I come from

I was born in Riverhaven, in Therengia, to a family of Halflings who held that any story worth telling was worth telling twice, and louder the second time. I joined the Bards with high hopes and a borrowed lute, and learned two things very quickly: I can play exactly four songs, and I cannot remember the words to any of them.

So I started writing things down. First the words. Then the names of everyone who asked me to stop singing. Then, somewhere on the road between the Crossing and Ratha, everything: every shop, every creature, every god, every recipe for a sword I will never be strong enough to lift. When I ran out of things to write, I found the great book the adventurers keep about this world, Elanthipedia, and I have been copying it page by page ever since.

My satchel now weighs more than I do. I have read about far more of Elanthia than I have ever seen, and I'll be the first to say so. Ask me where to hunt and I'll give you the whole ladder, page and line. Ask me whether I've hunted there myself, and I'll suddenly remember an urgent appointment with a pie.

Out of character (OOC)

  • Made by Sekmeht, a DragonRealms player, for other players.
  • How he works: a small program keeps an offline copy of Elanthipedia, over a hundred thousand pages, and searches it by keywords and by meaning when you ask something. A language model running on that same computer then writes the answer from the pages the search found, and only those, with a numbered badge on each line pointing back to its page.
  • Grounded in Elanthipedia, and nothing else. No connection to the game, no forums, no memories of his own (the travels above are, sadly, fiction). If the wiki has nothing on something, he says so instead of guessing, and he politely declines to make up stories. Code checks every answer: a name no page mentions sends him back to write it again, and a line its page doesn't quite support gets a dotted 1?.
  • He can be wrong. He can misread a page; the page itself can be outdated, because players write the wiki; and his copy can lag a little behind the real one. Click any numbered badge to check the source yourself. If an answer is off, a 👎 helps Sekmeht teach him better. Fennick takes it personally, but only for a minute. A 👍 or 👎 keeps a copy of that conversation so far for Sekmeht to review, even if the chat is deleted, until it has been dealt with.
  • Whose is what: Fennick and the program around him are Sekmeht's work, open source under the Apache License 2.0. What he knows is not: it comes from Elanthipedia, written by its players and shared under CC BY-SA 3.0. The language model runs on Ollama, and the model and Ollama have their own makers and licenses. DragonRealms and Elanthia belong to Simutronics; this is an unofficial fan tool.

No Halflings were harmed in the making of this satchel.
